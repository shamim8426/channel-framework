# AI Pandit - channel rules

Appended AFTER the global `RULES.md`. A channel may add or tighten rules, never loosen a global one.

## Format
- Vertical 9:16 short, 30-60 seconds, 80-160 words. One tip per video, no padding.
- Hook in the first 2 seconds: the problem or the payoff ("Claude Code me yeh ek command tumhara 1 ghanta bachayega").
- Structure: hook -> what it is (1 line) -> how to use it (2-4 concrete steps or one command) -> why it matters -> 1-line CTA.
- Every command, flag, file name or setting is shown as on-screen text exactly as typed (`/init`, `CLAUDE.md`, `claude mcp add`).
- Big word-by-word captions in the lower third, inside the safe area.

## Content
- Every technical claim is tagged to a dossier fact with at least 2 sources; official Anthropic docs/changelog count as the primary source.
- Features change fast: say which version/date the tip was checked against in the description. Unsure or deprecated = drop it.
- No fake screenshots or edited UI. Show the idea with the mascot and simple icons instead.
- Never use Anthropic's or any other company's logo or mascot; Jugnu is our own mascot.
- No hype claims ("replaces your whole team", "guaranteed income"). Show what it actually does.
- Never show real API keys, tokens or private paths, even fake-looking ones.

## Tone
- Hinglish: Hindi sentence flow in Roman script, technical words stay in English (prompt, terminal, MCP server, context window).
- Friendly "bhaiya/didi explaining to a friend" voice; short sentences; no jargon without a one-line explanation.

## Publishing
- Upload private; the owner watches and publishes.
- Description: the tip in 2 lines, the exact command(s), source links, "checked on <date>", `strings.ai_disclosure` and `strings.not_affiliated`.
